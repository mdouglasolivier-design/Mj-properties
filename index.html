About MJ Properties

Premium vacation rentals and real estate services in Kampala, Uganda

Our Story

Founded in 2018, MJ Properties started as a small family business with a passion for connecting travelers with unique accommodations in Kampala. Today, we've grown into a premier vacation rental and real estate service, offering exceptional properties across Uganda.

Our Mission

To provide unforgettable travel experiences through unique, comfortable, and well-maintained properties while delivering exceptional customer service that exceeds expectations.

Our Values

• Integrity in all our dealings
• Excellence in property management
• Customer satisfaction as our top priority
• Sustainable tourism practices
• Supporting local communities

Why Choose Us?

• Verified and quality-checked properties
• 24/7 customer support
• Competitive pricing
• Secure payment system
• Local expertise and recommendations
